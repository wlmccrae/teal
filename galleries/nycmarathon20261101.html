NYC Marathon 2026

New York, NY - 1 November 2026

For years I have said that one day I would get up early enough to see the elite marathon athletes in action at the front of the NYC Marathon.

This year, I did exactly that.

By 8:30am on the morning of the race, I was posted just past Mile 20 at 3rd Avenue and 140th Street in the Bronx. About 30 minutes later the elite wheelchair athletes began pushing through. I stayed out there for the next few hours, capturing all of the elite athletes as well as the first wave of the rest of the pack and the spectators cheering them on.

This is the 2026 New York City Marathon through my eyes.
