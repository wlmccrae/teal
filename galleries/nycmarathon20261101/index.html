NYC Marathon 2026

New York, NY - 1 November 2026

For years I have said that one day I would get up early enough to see the elite marathon athletes in action at the front of the NYC Marathon.

This year, I did exactly that.

By 8:30am on the morning of the race, I was posted just past Mile 20 at 3rd Avenue and 140th Street in the Bronx. About 30 minutes later the elite wheelchair athletes began pushing through. I stayed out there for the next few hours, capturing all of the elite athletes as well as the first wave of the rest of the pack and the spectators cheering them on.

This is the 2026 New York City Marathon through my eyes.

Switzerland's Marcel Hug pushes just ahead of American Daniel Romanchuk on 3rd Avenue at 140th Street in the Bronx, past mile 20 of the professional men's wheelchair race at the 2026 TCS New York City Marathon on November 1, 2026. The two traded the lead throughout the race. Hug won in 1:28:14, his eighth New York title, finishing about one second ahead of Romanchuk.

Great Britain's David Weir pushes up 3rd Avenue at 140th Street in the Bronx, with the Netherlands' Lito Anker right behind him, just past mile 20 of the professional men's wheelchair race at the 2026 TCS New York City Marathon on November 1, 2026. Weir held on for 3rd in 1:35:38, one second ahead of Anker in 4th. Switzerland's Marcel Hug won the race in 1:28:14, his eighth New York title.

Switzerland's Catherine Debrunner pushes alone up 3rd Avenue at 140th Street in the Bronx, just past mile 20 of the professional women's wheelchair race at the 2026 TCS New York City Marathon on November 1, 2026. Debrunner won in 1:40:02, more than eight minutes ahead of runner-up Tatyana McFadden (USA). It was her second New York title, after her first win in 2023. She also won earlier majors this year. Wikipedia lists her as the women's wheelchair winner at both Tokyo (1:37:15) and London (1:38:29) in 2026.

Defending champion Susannah Scaroni (USA) pushes up 3rd Avenue at 140th Street in the Bronx, with fellow American Tatyana McFadden close behind, just past mile 20 of the professional women's wheelchair race at the 2026 TCS New York City Marathon on November 1, 2026. McFadden went on to finish 2nd in 1:48:28, four seconds ahead of Scaroni in 3rd. Switzerland's Catherine Debrunner won the race in 1:40:02.

Australia's Madison de Rozario pushes alone up 3rd Avenue at 140th Street in the Bronx, just past mile 20 of the professional women's wheelchair race at the 2026 TCS New York City Marathon on November 1, 2026. De Rozario finished 4th in 1:53:06. Switzerland's Catherine Debrunner won the race in 1:40:02.

Eight of the top nine women in the 2026 TCS New York City Marathon run shoulder to shoulder on 3rd Avenue at 140th Street in the Bronx, just past mile 20 on November 1, 2026. Sheila Chepkirui (KEN) won in 2:24:36, her second New York title in three years. Jess McClain (USA) took second, becoming the first American woman on the New York podium since 2018, and defending champion Hellen Obiri (KEN) was third. Also in the pack: Edna Kiplagat (KEN), Mercy Chelangat (KEN), Carrie Ellwood (USA), Weini Kelati (USA) and Viola Cheptoo (KEN).

The elite men's lead pack heads down 3rd Avenue at 140th Street in the Bronx, just past mile 20 of the 2026 TCS New York City Marathon on November 1, 2026. All ten eventual top finishers are still running together here. Andreas Almgren (SWE) went on to win in 2:06:01 in his marathon debut. Yemaneberhan Crippa (ITA) was second and Kamohelo Mofolo (LES) was third. Benson Kipruto (KEN), Haftu Strintzos (AUS), Zouhair Talbi (USA), Ahmed Muhumed (USA), Geoffrey Kamworor (KEN), Ryota Kondo (JPN) and Ryan Ford (USA) completed the top ten.
